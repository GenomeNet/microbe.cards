Motivation

The microbial world is extraordinarily diverse, with millions of species spanning various ecosystems. While some are well-studied, many remain poorly characterized. Traditional literature-based annotation, though rigorous, often struggles to keep pace with rapid discoveries. microbe.cards leverages large language models (LLMs) to bridge these knowledge gaps, providing actionable insights that enhance scientific understanding.

What is microbe.cards?

microbe.cards integrates literature-based annotations, AI-driven predictions, and taxonomy insights into a single platform. By refining searches with advanced filters and examining model performance metrics, researchers can uncover patterns, test hypotheses, and deepen their understanding of microbial diversity.

Developed at the Helmholtz Centre for Infection Research, microbe.cards employs LLMs and data integration techniques to provide a dynamic, accessible, and continuously updated microbial phenotype database.

Our Focus

Data Curation

Blending established literature-based knowledge with LLM-derived predictions to maintain a comprehensive resource of microbial phenotypes.

Innovation

Employing LLMs and machine learning to reveal hidden patterns, guide future research, and enhance taxonomic and functional insights.

Accessibility

Providing open-access tools and data, enabling researchers worldwide to leverage microbial phenotypes for diverse scientific applications.
