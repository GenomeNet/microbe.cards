Microbial Impact Rankings

These rankings provide insight into the scientific influence and research interest surrounding various microbes. We periodically compute several metrics to help interpret a microbe's impact:

  • H-Index: A measure that attempts to reflect both the productivity and impact of a microbe's body of literature. A microbe with an H-index of h has h manuscripts each cited at least h times.
  • Total Citations: The sum of all citations across all manuscripts associated with the microbe.
  • Recent Citations (3 yrs): The citations received by a microbe's manuscripts in the last three years, showing current and emerging research interest.

All these values are precomputed by our system daily, ensuring that you see up-to-date metrics without long loading times. By examining the top performers in terms of H-index, total citations, and recent citations, you can quickly identify established "star" microbes and those currently gaining momentum in the scientific community.
