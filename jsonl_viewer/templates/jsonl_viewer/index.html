Welcome to microbe.cards

microbe.cards provides a synoptic view of microbial phenotypes. Explore literature-based annotations, AI-driven predictions, and taxonomy-driven insights. Dive into model performance metrics, discover connections between taxa, and refine your search with advanced filters. The information provided by AI models is for informational purposes only and may contain inaccuracies or errors. It should not be used for medical diagnosis, treatment, or any health-related decision-making. Consult professional sources and model performance metrics for accuracy assessment.
